## I didn't always think like this
<p> During my studies and recent family hardships It's hard to think rationally and or normally without rash and unmeant feeling to become a reality. </p>

<i> Everyone has a monster inside of them and they are held accountable for it when it breaks free </i>

<p> The reality is that life is not about the fancy cars, the mansion at the top of the hill, the party lifestyle... 

What are you really doing?

What are you accomplishing?

I used to ask these same type of questions and during my early and late years of college I was making so much money that I had never seen before. I was not familiar to the concept of having more than even 1000 dollars....

The truth about people who earn money quickly and lack the ability to fully understand how to properly use it is so true. It could not be more true. If you win 500,000 and have only every been used to minimum wage... you will spend it very stupidly and not fully understand it's power and weight.

It took me 4 years to fully understand these following things:

1. The money isn't coming with you to the grave.

2. On your death bed, you won't be wishing you had a couple more million... you'll be wishing you had a couple more seconds with your kids and more importantly your wife.

3. Finding ways to make money while not working should be everyone's main goal.

### You will notice I put my wife above my children

<i> I don't even have kids yet </i> 

... but I know that when I do, I would always put my wife slightly higher on the priority list. If they things aren't right there how can I expect my children to see me at my best.

## So what's the purpose of this entry into my journal

<p> The intention is to create a reminder to myself to always do these things.... no matter if I am tired, irritated, or feeling like I am doing nothing right at all. </p>

### So here's the 7 rules for Benjamin

1. Work to love and love to work

2. No amount of money ever bought a second of time - Thanks Iron Man

3. Your role model should be you in 10 years. Chase it. Live it.

4. Don't half ass it. <i> Now even I can't fold a shirt properly... but if I wanna go out and try something and or use up my time then I wanna do it at 150% not 50%. </i>

5. Be someone admirable and adore your significant other.

6. Talk less and listen more.

7. Don't talk about your ideas... execute them.

## I am no genius... this list will change

<i> Money alleviates stress, but it doesn't fix someone who's unwilling to change. </i>
