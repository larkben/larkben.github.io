## You can become wealthy doing anything

<i> Becoming a wealthy individual just means you can live the lifestyle you desire comfortably. </i>

<p> The millionaires that I know and have studied, didn't become millionaires because they chose to go school for many years, but because they worked hard at the job they had. They were resourceful and cheap. They understood the value and the worth of the dollar. They often invested in assets that were risk adverse and they knew alot about rather than what people told them were good investable assets. </p>

<i> A computer scientist will likely not choose to invest in automotive vehicles or land. ~ They like computers and technology so they will invest in stocks and or build apps that produce income. </i>

<i> A teacher will invest in their students, their ~pension, and their education. ~ Education has a direct impact on their earning potential. ~ Bonus: Summers off. </i>

<i> A doctor will invest their time into their job and helping others. Due to the little time they may have, their time to invest or spend time on other ventures is limited. ~ You can make alot as a doctor though. </p>

<i> A designer will invest in their brand. Their recognition and their status allows their earning potential to directly increase in relation to the quality of their work. ~ Marketing / Web Design / Clothing ... huge industries with untapped potential and requires tapping into the minds of people. </p>

<i> A farmer will invest in land, cattle, machinery and anything that allows them to farm increase their earning potential and or the amount of acres they can farm. ~ Farmers have no real retirement, so in addition to being a farmer you have to be financially savy and resourceful with every dollar. </i>
