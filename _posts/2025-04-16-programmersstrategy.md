## Stocks for normies
<i> You lose all your money.... </i>
</br>
<p> Well actually losing all your money buying just stocks is quite hard if you stick companies like Microsoft and Apple. You lose all your money when you start playing with options, and other financial tools that brokerages give to people way too easily in my opinion. </p>
</br>
<p> The truth is that you are probably not capable of managing a portfolio. Index(es) and Mutual fund managers are paid big money to do this for you. If they don't manage funds well... they don't get their stupid sized bonus and they are replaced with some other guy who promises higher returns. </p>
</br>
<p> Start investing early and with as much as you can. It's a game about compunding value not about what stocks you choose. </p>

### My Plan
<p> 13% of bi-weekly income goes towards funding my retirement. Rebalanced among 50% large caps, 25% mid caps, and 25% small caps. Sit back and don't look at it for a long time. </p>

## Money and it's worth
<i> You probably have a false sense of the power associated with a crisp $1 bill. </i>
</br>
<p> Their is a saying that I like and intend to live by. Money talks but wealth whipsers. The more cushioned I become and the more I endulge myself in this capitalistic society I am reminded that their is no amount of money that will make you happy forever. Money provides what I like to 
call temporary happiness. It's true If I won the lottery I would continue working and I wouldn't tell anyone I won. My kid would drive a shit car that's 20 - 25 years old and I would put him to work. Life doesn't chnage for me. I just have better tools at my disposal. </p>
</br>
<p> I find myself most happy when I am able to provide for those closet to me without the worry of tomorrow's unexpected expenses and or expected bills... or the next weeks... or the next months. That's what money is buying you. Peace of mind. Freedom. The simple concept of having
money at your disposal or at your deployment in a moments notice is powerful on the mind. Don't let anyone tell you it isn't. </p>
